# AURA Workspace Safety & Ergonomic Guidelines

## 1. Ergonomic Best Practices
- Screen Position: Position the monitor directly in front of you at eye level, approximately an arm's length away.
- Seating Posture: Sit with your back straight against the chair support, keeping your feet flat on the floor.
- Keyboard and Mouse: Keep wrists in a neutral, relaxed position to reduce repetitive strain.

## 2. Electrical Safety
- Never overload electrical outlets with multiple high-draw power adapters.
- Keep cups, water bottles, and open liquid containers away from power cords and electronic equipment.
- Inspect cables regularly for signs of wear or exposed wiring.

## 3. Visual Rest and Lighting
- Follow the 20-20-20 rule: Every 20 minutes, look at an object at least 20 feet away for 20 seconds.
- Ensure ambient room lighting is balanced to prevent glare and reduce optical eye strain.
