# AURA Workstation & Laptop User Manual

## 1. System Overview
The AURA Laptop Computer is a high-performance portable workstation designed for visual artificial intelligence processing, edge neural network inference, and multimodal reasoning.

## 2. Power and Thermal Management
- To turn on the laptop, press the power button located at the top-right corner of the keyboard deck.
- Always operate the laptop on a hard, flat surface (such as a desk) to maintain proper airflow and ventilation.
- Ensure the cooling vents located along the bottom and rear edges are clear of obstruction.

## 3. Peripheral Connections
- USB-C Thunderbolt 4: Connect external 4K monitors, high-speed camera adapters, and docking stations.
- HDMI 2.1: Direct video output to projectors and auxiliary displays.
- Audio Jack: 3.5mm headphone and microphone combo port for high-fidelity voice interaction.

## 4. Maintenance and Care
- Clean the display using a dry microfiber cloth. Do not apply liquid cleaners directly to the screen.
- Shut down the device cleanly before transporting it in a backpack or carrying case.
